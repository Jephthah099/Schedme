1SCHUDME — time planner for medical students
Brief · assumptions

Year-3 medical student. Fixed timetable (lectures, labs, ward rounds) with self-directed study wedged between. The app opens on progress, not the calendar: the question is "am I hitting my study hours", the schedule is how you answer it.

Four notification jobs: reminder before a block, nudge when drifting off schedule, end-of-day recap, weekly digest. Copy is neutral and precise — no cheerleading.

System
Surfaces
Soft white cards on the warm ground; 24px corners, hairline rules, no heavy boxes.
Category fills
Lecture = solid ink · Lab = outlined · Clinical = accent field · Study = accent tint.
Accent
Red carries the primary action, the now-line and the one poster surface (1c).
Charts
Outline = planned, solid = actual; round-capped bars and rings.
Photography
Grayscale only, through the image wrapper.

Notes
Progress first. The hero is one number: hours against today's target. Everything under it is evidence.
Outline vs solid. Planned is drawn, actual is filled — no colour coding to learn.
Drag on Today. Snaps to 15 minutes, clamped 06:00–22:00, and confirms with the banner the real reminder would use.
Photo slots. The two grayscale panels are empty until you drop real photographs on them.
